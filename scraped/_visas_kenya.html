Kenya Tourist Visa Requirements

To apply for Kenya e-visa, following documents are required in high definition scan.

  1. Passport first and second page. Validity must be at least 6 months from date of travel.
  2. NIC front and back
  3. Passport size photo with white background
  4. Confirmed return air ticket
  5. Confirmed hotel booking
  6. Bank statement of last 6 months is recommended
  7. Itinerary of your trip to Kenya with detail of places to visit

Best Time to Travel to Kenya for Tourism

The best time to travel to Kenya is from June to October and January to February during dry season. You can enjoy the best views of wildlife during this time.

If you want to witness the great migration of Maasai Mara, you should travel during June to October when millions of wildebeest, zebras, and other animals cross the Mara River from Tanzania's Serengeti into Kenya's Maasai Mara, creating an amazing sight for visitors.

Top Tourist Attractions in Kenya

If you are planning a visit to Kenya, we recommend adding following to your travel itinerary to get the most out of your visit.

  • Maasai Mara National Reserve – World-famous for the Great Migration
  • Amboseli National Park – Stunning views of Mount Kilimanjaro and large elephant herds
  • Lake Nakuru National Park – Home to flamingos, rhinos, and diverse bird species
  • Nairobi National Park – A unique safari experience just outside Kenya’s capital city
  • Diani Beach – Pristine white sand beach with crystal-clear waters and water sports

There are also attractions and activities for kids if you are traveling with family.

  • Giraffe Centre (Nairobi) – Kids can feed and interact with endangered Rothschild giraffes
  • Nairobi Safari Walk – A fun, safe way for kids to see Kenya’s wildlife up close

How to Apply your Kenya e-visa

The Kenya e-visa is called eTA or electronic travel authority that can be applied through their official website https://etakenya.go.ke

Following are the steps to apply your eTA to Kenya from Pakistan.

  1. Open the Kenya immigration official website and from home page click 'Apply Now'
  2. Select 'Tourists & Visitors' category
  3. You will be shown the check list of required documents
  4. Accept the terms and click 'Continue' to start your application
  5. On next screen click 'Individual applicant' or other applicable category to you
  6. Select country of residence 'Pakistan' and click continue
  7. You will be taken to step by step process of entering following requirements
    1. Passport Information
    2. Selfie or Photo
    3. Contact Information
    4. Trip Information
    5. Travel Information
    6. Customs Declaration
    7. Required Documents
    8. Confirm and Proceed
    9. Payment

Complete the step by step process and make payment on final step to complete your application. On approval, your eTA will be delivered to you through email.

Frequently Asked Questions on Kenya eTA from Pakistan

How long can I stay in Kenya on my eTA

Kenya eTA comes with 3 months validity. Travelers are allowed to stay maximum 30 days in the country on their tourist visa.

Can I extend my tourist visa once I arrive in Kenya

The extension of your tourist visa is not usually allowed. In case of any emergency, visitors must immediately contact Kenya immigration related to their stay.

What is Kenya eTA approval rate for Pakistani citizens

The approval rate for Kenya eTA or e-visa is moderately better for Pakistani nationals. It is highly recommended to apply your visa through a tourism company for better chances of your application approval.

What is the processing time of the visa application

Your visa application usually takes 3 to 4 working days. This may take a week or 10 days some times. Please make sure to book your tickets after approval of your visa.
