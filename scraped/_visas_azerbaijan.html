A blend of European and Asian culture and architect, Azerbaijan is a must visit destination. The capital city Baku is full of historical places and delicious cuisines.

Requirements for Azerbaijan Visa

Azerbaijan issues an e-visa for Pakistani nationals which is very easy to obtain. Following are requirements to apply the visa. Only clear high definition scan of following documents is required (no need to submit original documents).

  1. First page of passport that must be valid at least 6 months from the date of travel
  2. Scan of CNIC - front and back
  3. Address in Azerbaijan where you will be staying during your visit (hotel address)

Note: Azeri Immigration recommends that travelers should get a travel insurance before they travel. Book your travel insurance to Azerbaijan with 16% discount.

Best Time to Visit Baku

Types and Validity of Azerbaijan Visas

The most common visa types are tourist or business visit. A visa with 90 days from the date of your given departure is issued. The travelers can stay maximum 30 days from the date of their arrival into the country. It is recommended that travelers must exit Azerbaijan at least 1 day before their visa expiry.

Eligibility Criteria for Pakistani Nationals

The visa requirements are straight forward and travelers can apply their visas directly on the official website without submitting original passport or documents. Azerbaijan immigration has made it very easy for Pakistani nationals to obtain tourist and visa with high ratio of visa approvals.

Steps to Apply Azerbaijan Visa Online

To apply Azerbaijan e-visa, go to https://evisa.gov.az from from a desktop browser. This is official and only authentic website to apply your e-visa.

  1. From the home page select 'Application' menu
  2. On next page select nationality 'Pakistan', travel document 'Ordinary' for regular passport, number of entries 'Single' and type the verification code in the box and click 'Continue'
  3. On next page select purpose of your visit 'tourism', select mode 'Normal' or 'Urgent', and then starting date of your travel at least 2 days before your intended day of departure and click 'Continue'
  4. Read, accept and understand all notices
  5. Fill out easy to complete visa application form with correct detail
  6. Upload first page of your passport as per sample and click 'Complete application'

After completing your application you will receive a payment link in your email. Follow the link to complete payment through your debit or credit card. 

Your application is complete and your visa will be delivered to your email upon approval within the due time as per your select mode normal or urgent.

What to Expect from your Visit to Azerbaijan

Azerbaijan is a rich blend of Islamic traditions and secular influences. Islam, primarily Shia, is the dominant religion, but the city is a perfect example of religious tolerance and cultural diversity. Azerbaijani culture is a mix of of Persian, Turkish, and Russian influences.

The cuisine is a flavorful mix of Eastern and Western flavors, with popular dishes like Plov (rice pilaf), Kebabs, Dolma, and Kutabs, often accompanied by aromatic teas and local sweets like Bakhlava. The fast food chains offer a great value for money on burgers and pizzas. Also you can find Indian or Pakistani restaurants in the streets.

Frequently Asked Questions for Azerbaijan e-Visa

How long can I stay in Azerbaijan with my tourist visa

Your tourist visa allows you to stay maximum 30 days from the date of your arrival in the country.

Can I extend my tourist visa once I reach Azerbaijan

Extension of your tourist visa is usually not permitted. In case of any exceptional scenarios, travelers must contact the immigration department for review.

Can I enter multiple times in Azerbaijan multiple times on my tourist visa

Your tourist visa is only valid for a single entry. Once entered in the country, your visa is considered as used and is no more valid for re-entry even if it is within 30 days. Travelers must re-apply for a new visa in such cases.

Do I need a visa if I am transiting through Baku

Visa is not required if you are transiting through Heydar Aliyev International Airport, Baku without leaving the airport and your transit in Baku is not more than 24 hours. For all other scenarios, passengers must obtain a visa.

Which are major airports in Azerbaijan where I can travel from Pakistan

Baku (GYD) airport is currently the only airport where all major airlines operate to and from Pakistan through direct or connecting flights.
