Adamjee Insurance Policy for Family Travel Insurance

Adamjee family travel insurance plan is valid for husband, wife under the age of 65 and unlimited children under between the ages of 6 months and 18 years each. Family travel insurance plan can also be booked for single parent traveling with children.

Adamjee Insurance Age Surcharges

The individual travel insurance prices are for travelers up to age of 65 years. For ages between 65 and 85, the insurance price is increased by 100% (doubled)
