Currency Export Rules in Pakistan for International Travel (Updated 2026)

  Last updated on 10 Sep, 2026

How much currency I can take with me while traveling abroad from Pakistan? How much cigarettes I can take with me? Learn about export limits and regulations while traveling on international flights from Pakistan.

Everything you need to know about:

  1. Foreign currency export limits
  2. Rules on carrying cigarettes
  3. Export regulations on other common items
  4. How to declare currency at departure

1. Limits on Export of Foreign Currency on International Flights

Pakistani nationals can carry foreign currency with maximum limits as per following table. The foreign currency can be US Dollar (USD) or any other well recognized foreign currency - but the value of foreign currency should not exceed the equivalent value of USD limits.

Passenger Types Per Trip Limit Total Annual Limit
18 Years and above USD 5,000 USD 30,000
Under 18 years USD 2,500 USD 15,000

Important things to remember for travelers with foreign currency:

  • The currency must be acquired through an authentic and licensed currency exchange company
  • Travelers must keep the original receipt of currency exchange to present at customs
  • Export of Pak Rupees (PKR) is prohibited
  • Keep your cash in a well locked carry-on or hand bag while traveling
  • Always keep an eye throughout your journey on the bags having cash and your travel documents
  • Keep a copy of your passport with you and come to airport a bit more early as currency declaration formalities may take an additional 15 to 20 minutes

2. Limit on Export of Cigarettes or Tobacco from Pakistan

As per customs regulations, passengers of age 18 years and above can carry cigarettes or tobacco within following limits. Travelers can choose any one of following options (not all of them together). 

  • Maximum 200 cigarettes
  • or 50 cigars
  • or half pound of tobacco

3. Export Regulations on Other Common Items

There are other significant points to remember related to export regulations while traveling abroad from Pakistan..

  • Export of antiquities is prohibited to foreign nationals and nationals of Pakistan residing abroad who pay a short visit to Pakistan.
  • For crew members: customs allowances are very restricted. Firearms, alcohol and plants are prohibited.

4. How to Declare Foreign Currency at Departure

Passengers can now pre-declare their foreign currency before travel using official "Pass Track" app available for smartphones. The app helps you reduce formalities at flight departure if you are carrying foreign currency.

Pass Track app also lets you keep track of your trips and calculate the remaining limits of carrying foreign currency on future trips.
