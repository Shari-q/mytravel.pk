What is the minimum coverage required for a Schengen visa from Pakistan?

All Schengen embassies require a minimum EUR 30,000 (approximately USD 50,000) emergency medical coverage for the full duration of your trip. All plans on MyTravel.pk meet this requirement and are accepted by every Schengen embassy operating in Pakistan.

Can one Schengen insurance policy cover multiple countries?

Yes. One Schengen insurance policy is valid across all 27 Schengen member states including Germany, France, Italy, Netherlands, Spain, Austria, Switzerland, and more. You do not need a separate policy for each country you visit.

Is Schengen travel insurance refundable if my visa is rejected?

Most travel insurance companies do not refund your travel insurance even in case of visa rejection. In case a company allows the refund, you are required to present original rejection letter from the embassy and a cancellation fee applies on refund if approved.

What documents are needed to buy Schengen travel insurance in Pakistan?

You need: (1) a clear passport scan or photo, (2) CNIC copy, (3) exact travel dates, (4) destination country, (5) nominee details, and (6) your residential address in Pakistan. The entire booking process takes less than 30 minutes.
