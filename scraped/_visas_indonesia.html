Requirements for Indonesia Tourist e-Visa

Indonesia also provides tourist e-visa facility. If you do not want to physically submit your passport, you can apply for Indonesia e-visa. The charges for e-visa are slightly higher. Following are the requirements to apply for e-visa.

Only high definition scan of following original documents is required.

  1. Passport first page having validity of more than 6 months from the date of intended travel
  2. Passport size photograph with white background and dark shirt/dress
  3. Bank statement of last 3 months
  4. CNIC front and back scan
  5. Family relationship certificate (FRC) and marriage registration certificate (MRC) required for all travelers where applicable

Indonesia Arrival Customs Declaration - Mandatory

All passengers entering Indonesia must complete a Customs Declaration Form (BC 2.2) for themselves and their families, ensuring accurate and truthful information. The form should be filled online at https://ecd.beacukai.go.id

Prohibited items include narcotics, weapons, and goods violating national laws. Cash or payment instruments exceeding Indonesia Rupiah 100 million (~ USD 6,000) must be declared, and foreign currencies above Indonesia Rupiah 1 billion (~ USD 60,000) require Bank Indonesia's permit. Import duty exemptions apply for personal goods, with set limits for passengers, crew, and excisable items like alcohol and tobacco. Goods beyond limits, trade-related imports, or undeclared electronics must be reported, or they may face duties, confiscation, or destruction. Incorrect or incomplete declarations are subject to legal sanctions.

Best Time to Visit Indonesia for Tourism

The ideal time to visit Indonesia for tourism is during the dry season between May to September. During dry season the weather is warm and sunny with less humidity making it ideal for outdoor activities and beach going.

If you prefer less crowded places and don't mind occasional rains, the months of April and October are also good choice. The rainy season runs from November to March that brings frequent rain and downpours.

What to Expect when Visiting Indonesia

When visiting Indonesia, expect a diverse cultural experience influenced by Islam, Hinduism, and Buddhism, with mosques, temples, and traditional ceremonies found across the islands. Indonesian cuisine is flavorful, featuring dishes like nasi goreng, satay, and rendang, often served with spicy sambal.

Halal food is easily available also in fast food chains. The people are known for their warm hospitality and friendly, making visitors feel welcome wherever they go.

Types of Indonesian Visas and Processing Time

Embassy of Indonesia currently offers sticker and e-visas to Pakistani nationals. The process of obtaining sticker visa involves physical documentation and paperwork and your original passport is submitted to the embassy.

Meanwhile obtaining e-visa requires less requirements, paperwork and no need to submit your passport physically. Processing time of visa application varies between one to two weeks depending on the nature of your visa. 

Frequently Asked Questions Related to Indonesia Visa in Pakistan

How long can I stay in Indonesia on my tourist visa

Indonesian tourist visa comes with 3 months validity. Travelers are allowed to stay maximum 30 days in Indonesia on their tourist visa.

Can I extend my tourist visa once I arrive in Indonesia

The extension of your tourist visa is not usually allowed. On exceptional cases, travelers must immediately contact Indonesian immigration authorities for help.

Do I need a travel insurance when visiting Indonesia

Travel insurance is not required by the immigration when traveling to Indonesia, however travelers are recommended to obtain a travel insurance for worry free travel. You can check travel insurance prices here.

Do I require any vaccination before traveling

There are no requirements for polio or any other kind of vaccination before traveling to Indonesia.

What is Indonesian visa approval rate for Pakistani citizens

The visa approval rate is quite good for Pakistani nationals. If you are a genuine travel with a basic previous travel history, the chances of your Indonesia visa getting approved are high.
