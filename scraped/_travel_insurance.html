Is family travel insurance available from Pakistan?

Yes. Select the "Family" option in the booking form to get coverage for multiple family members under a single policy. This is more economical than buying individual policies for each family member.

What documents are required to buy travel insurance?

You need: (1) Passport scan or clear photo, (2) CNIC copy, (3) Travel dates, (4) Destination country name, (5) Detail of the nominee person, and (6) Residential address in Pakistan.

Is travel insurance refundable in case of visa rejection?

Travel insurance policies once issued are generally not refundable. Most travel insurance companies do not refund the travel insurance even in case of visa rejection.
