Malaysia is an ideal Asian destination for family holidays. Enjoy the tropical weather with friends and family and spend your next vacation in Malaysia.

Requirements for Malaysia e-Visa

Only high definition scan is required for following documents. Original documents are not required for Malaysia e-visa application.

  1. First and second page of passport (must be valid at least 7 months from the date of departure)
  2. In case of any previous/expired passport(s), scan of front page of previous passport(s)
  3. CNIC front and back
  4. Bank statement of last 6 months with minimum balance of PKR 500,000
  5. Photo with white background and dark shirt
  6. Birth certificate of minor applicants required
  7. FRC (family registration certificate) and/or Marriage certificate is required for families.

Malaysia Digital Arrival Card (MDAC)

All visitors traveling to Malaysia are required to complete Malaysia Digital Arrival Card or MDAC online 3 days prior to their arrival. This is a mandatory process to complete before your arrival to avoid any inconvenience during your travel.

To fill your digital arrival card, visit official website https://imigresen-online.imi.gov.my/mdac/main

Ideal Time to Visit Malaysia

If you are traveling to Malaysia for vacations, the weather has different patterns in east and west coasts and generally March to early Octobers are ideal.

For Kuala Lumpur, Penang and Langkawi which are most visited tourist cities, the best time is usually between December to March. The weather during this time is dry and sunny with temperatures between 25°C to 32°C. However due to its tropical weather, travelers can travel throughout the year.

How to Apply Malaysia Visa Online

Malaysian e-visa can be applied through Malaysia Immigration's official website https://malaysiavisa.imi.gov.my

Applicants need to sign up for an account and then start a visa application. Once you have created your account and verified through your email, you can start your application.

The application process is simple and self explanatory. You will need to upload scan of your passport, Pakistani national identity card (NIC), bank statement and a hotel reservation. Upon approval, you are notified through email to download your e-visa.

What to Expect when Visiting Malaysia

Malaysia is a vibrant multicultural nation with a rich blend of Malay, Chinese, Indian, and indigenous traditions. Islam is the official religion, but the country embraces religious diversity, with mosques, temples, and churches coexisting.

The major cuisines are Malay, Chinese, and Indian, with famous dishes like Nasi Lemak and Char Kway Teow. Malaysians are known for their hospitality and friendliness.

Important Things to Remember while Traveling from Pakistan

When departing from Pakistan, you should expect some checks at departure and arrival airports. Travelers especially who are traveling for the first time to any international destination or have very limited travel history, must carry sufficient money (at least $75 to $100 per day) to cover their stay in Malaysia.

Travelers are also required to have a confirmed and paid hotel reservation for their entire stay. A confirmed return ticket is also mandatory for all visitors.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the processing time of Malaysia e-Visa

Your visa application is usually processed within 3 to 5 working days. Applicants are notified through email about status of their visa application.

What are different types of Malaysian visas

Travelers can apply for tourist or business visit visas. A tourist visa is usually applied for minimum documentations. Your Malaysian tourist visa comes with 6 months validity. Travelers can stay in Malaysia for maximum 30 days during this validity.

Can I extend my tourist visa after arriving in Malaysia

A tourist visa is not extendable. Your 30 days duration of stay gives you sufficient time to explore the country. Visitors must exit the country within the validity of your visa.

Can I work in Malaysia on visit visa

Working or finding employment in Malaysia on a visitors visa is strictly prohibited. A visit visa is issued purely for the purpose of tourism to the travelers.

What are common causes of visa rejections

The most common causes of visa rejections are invalid or low balance bank statements, improper form filing, providing false information or attaching unjustified hotel reservations.

What are approval rate of Malaysian visas for Pakistani nationals

Malaysia visa approval rate for Pakistani nationals is quite good but few applications may get rejected. The decision is purely made by Malaysian immigration and the reason is not given in the application response in case of rejection.
