Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Will I get guaranteed visa approval

Visa approval is always subject to embassy's decision. However a professionally completed visa application improves the chances of visa approval. We mention the approval ratio of each visa in the description. Please refer to visa information pages for detailed information.

Is my visa fee refundable in case of visa rejection

Visa fees are paid to embassies in advance and are not refunded in case of visa rejection. Most of the embassies also do not provide a reason or evidence of rejection in case of a visa application does not succeed.

Can I apply for student or work visa with you

We do not deal in any kind of student, work or non tourist category visas. We only deal in tourist visas mentioned on our website. We do not provide information on any kind of visa that is not listed on our website.

How long does the visa processing take

Visa processing time varies depending on the country, embassy workload, and travel season. Normally, it can take anywhere between 5 to 20 working days. We recommend applying at least 3 to 4 weeks before your planned travel date to avoid delays. Each visa page on our website mentions the estimated processing duration.

Do I need to provide confirmed flight and hotel bookings

Most embassies require proof of travel arrangements such as flight reservations and hotel bookings. However, confirmed tickets are usually not mandatory before visa approval. Our team can assist you with visa-compliant flight and hotel bookings that meet embassy requirements.

Can I reapply if my visa is rejected

Yes, you can reapply for a visa after a rejection. It is important to review the possible reasons for refusal and ensure that missing or incorrect information is corrected in your new application. Our experts can guide you on preparing a stronger application for better chances of approval.
