Visa Requirements for Singapore Tourist Visa

Singapore issues an electronic visa (e-visa) to tourists. Following documents are required to apply for Singapore visit visa.

  1. Passport scan (first two pages) with minimum 6 months validity from the date of travel
  2. Copy of CNIC (front and back)
  3. Copy of NTN
  4. Bank statement of last 6 months and account maintenance letter (stamped from the bank)
  5. Visa request on letterhead
  6. Passport size photograph with white background and dark shirt (without glasses)
  7. FRC (family relation certificate) and MRC (marriage registration certificate) where applicable
  8. Form-B for any children traveling with family

Singapore Visa Types & Processing Time

It takes 7 to 10 working days in visa processing when you apply from Pakistan. In some cases the processing may take up to 2 weeks. There is standard single entry tourist e-visa that is issued to tourist from Pakistan. The tourist visa comes with 2 months validity and maximum 30 days stay entitlement.

Prohibition on Electronic Cigarettes, Vaporisers and Chewing Tobacco

Singapore has implemented a strict prohibition on e-cigarettes, vaporizers and any kind of chiewing tobacco such as nicotine pouches and similar products. Carrying such items while you enter Singapore may result in penalties and deportation.

SG Arrival Card (SGAC) with Electronic Health Declaration

Travelers are required to submit the SG Arrival Card with Electronic Health Declaration before arriving in Singapore within 3 days before arrival to avoid unnecessary delays during immigration clearance.

The declaration can be completed easily on official website by visiting https://eservices.ica.gov.sg/sgarrivalcard/

Best Time to Visit Singapore

Due to mostly warm and humid climate, the best time to visit Singapore is between February to April when it's dry and less humid. The temperature ranges between 25°C to 31°C during these months. However due to modern infrastructure of the country, travelers can visit anytime during the year.

Eligibility Criteria for Pakistani Nationals

There is only basic documentation requirements when applying for your Singapore visit visa. However it is recommended that you apply your visa with family for better chances of approval. Individual Pakistani citizens applying for the visa may may be required to provide additional documents.

Double Entry Singapore Visa for Cruise Packages

Singapore is home to one of the best luxury & premium cruise operators including Royal Caribbean, Princess Cruises and Celebrity Cruises. There are also several European and regional cruise operators that operate to and from Singapore.

If you are planning to book luxury but affordable cruise package from Pakistan, Singapore is the place to start. In order to book a round-trip cruise package that departs and ends at Singapore, you need to have a multiple tourist visa.

What to Expect from your Visit to Singapore

Singapore is a multicultural city-state with a rich blend of Chinese, Malay, Indian, and Eurasian influences, reflected in its festivals, traditions, and daily life. With a population of around 5.9 million, Singapore is known for its modern infrastructure, cultural diversity, and vibrant lifestyle.

Singapore's MRT (Mass Rapid Transit) is has a wide network of public transport system, making it easy for commuters to travel across the city. The country has strict cleanliness regulations, including fines for littering, spitting, and chewing gum, ensuring a tidy and well-maintained urban environment.

Important Travel Advice 

Singapore immigration has strong profiling and checks policy on arrival for citizens of many countries including Pakistan. It is highly recommended that your visit to Singapore is well justified. You must have a confirmed return air ticket, a paid hotel reservation and at least $100 per day to cover your expenses during your visit.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I apply for Singapore visa online myself

Singapore currently does not offer direct visa applications by applicants in Pakistan. You need to apply through a sponsor or tour operator company

Can I extend my tourist visa once I reach Singapore

Singapore tourist visa is not extendable and tourists are required to leave the country withing their visa validity. The local authority has strict laws for people violating the immigration laws or overstaying

What is approval rate of Singapore visa for Pakistani citizens

If you are applying your visa with your family for the purpose of vacations, the approval ratio is usually very good. Individuals applying for the visa may have limited approval rate
