Vietnam is an excellent travel destination for Pakistani tourists seeking culture, history, nature, and affordable adventures. Vietnam offers a mix of ancient cities, colorful street life, and beautiful landscapes from mountains to beaches. A great choice for your Asian adventure.

Documents Required for Vietnam e-Visa

To apply for a Vietnam e-visa, only scanned copies of the following documents are required. Original documents are not required for submission of visa application.

  1. Scan of first page of passport. Must be valid at least 6 months from date of intended departure.
  2. Scan of NIC (national identity card) front and back.
  3. Passport size photograph in white background
  4. A request on letterhead for visa application. Issued by your employer if salaried, or on company's letterhead if self-employed

Vietnam Entry & Travel Requirements

  • All travelers must carry a printed copy of their approved e-visa.
  • You must show your return ticket and proof of accommodation at immigration.
  • Vietnam does not require a digital arrival card, but travelers may be asked to fill out health declaration forms if required at the time of travel.

Best Time to Visit Vietnam

Vietnam’s climate varies by region, but the best overall months to visit are:

  • March to May: Pleasant temperatures and less rainfall across most of the country.
  • October to December: Cooler weather and clearer skies in the north.

For beach lovers visiting Da Nang, Nha Trang, or Phu Quoc, the dry season between February and August is ideal.

How to Apply Vietnam Visa Online

You can apply for your Vietnam tourist e-visa through the official government portal at https://evisa.xuatnhapcanh.gov.vn online. Following are the steps to apply your visa.

  1. Visit the above website and click 'e-Visa for foreigners'.
  2. Fill out the visa application form.
  3. Upload your scanned passport, CNIC, photo, and travel documents.
  4. Pay the visa fee online via credit/debit card.

You will usually receive the visa approval by email within 3 to 7 working days.

Top Places to Visit in Vietnam

What to Expect When Visiting Vietnam

Vietnam is a culturally rich country with a blend of ancient traditions and modern urban life. The majority religion is Buddhism, but the country practices religious tolerance. Local food is a major highlight – don’t miss dishes like Pho, Banh Mi, and Fresh Spring Rolls. Vietnamese people are generally warm, helpful, and curious about foreigners, especially in less-touristy areas.

Important Things to Know When Traveling from Pakistan

  • Carry sufficient funds (at least $60 to $100 per day) to avoid issues at immigration.
  • Keep copies of hotel bookings and return flight ticket ready for inspection.
  • Avoid carrying large amounts of cash; carry an international debit or credit card if possible.
  • Dress modestly when visiting temples or rural areas.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does Vietnam e-Visa processing take?

Vietnam visa processing usually takes 1 to 2 weeks. On approval, you are notified through email and the visa is emailed to you as a PDF.

Can I work in Vietnam on a tourist visa?

No. Working on a tourist visa is illegal and may result in fines or deportation. 

What are common reasons for Vietnam visa rejection?

Common reasons include: Invalid or unclear passport scans, false or suspicious travel documents, insufficient bank balance and incomplete application or photo not meeting guidelines

Is Vietnam visa approval guaranteed for Pakistanis?

Approval is not guaranteed, but most applicants with proper documents and a valid travel purpose are approved without issue.
