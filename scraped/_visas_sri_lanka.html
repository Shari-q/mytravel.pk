Apply Sri Lanka Online e-Visa (ETA) from Pakistan

PKR12,900 e-Visa 30 Days Validity

Pakistani nationals require an electronic travel authority or ETA to travel to Sri Lanka. The ETA acts as an electronic visa (e-visa). Here is everything you need to know before you apply for visa to Sri Lanka.

Sri Lanka Entry Visa Requirements

Following documents are required to apply for your e-visa to Sri Lanka.

  1. High definition scan of first and second page of passport
  2. Photo in white background and dark shirt (without eye-glasses)

Sri Lanka Visa Fee

Sri Lanka entry visa is offered in following options and prices.

Visa Type Validity Maximum Stay Fee
Sri Lanka Double Entry Tourist Visa 1 Month 30 Days PKR 12,900

Best Time to Visit Sri Lanka for Vacations

The best time to visit Sri Lanka is dependent on the region. Most tourists usually travel to Colombo, Negombo and Kandy which are in west coast and hill country region. In this region December to April offers the best weather.

While the east coast and north are ideal from May to September. If you are traveling on budget, the shoulder seasons April and September–November offer moderate weather, fewer crowds, and lower prices.

Types of Sri Lanka Visas and Processing Times

The online visa application usually takes 2 to 3 days for approval. There are three major categories: Tourist, Business Visit and Transit visa. Fees vary depending on the nationality of the applicant.

Following are types of visas and their official fees for Pakistani nationals.

Visa Category No. of Entries Visa Validity Fee
Tourist Visa Double Entry 30 Days USD 20
Business Visa Multiple Entries 30 Days USD 30
Transit Visa Single Entry 2 Days Free

Transit visa is only applicable if you are traveling from Pakistan on Sri Lankan airlines with a long layover in Colombo (no more than 2 days) and then continuing your journey onward to a third country.

Steps to Apply Sri Lanka Electronic Travel Authorization (ETA)

In order to apply for your e-visa or ETA online, visit official website of Sri Lankan Electronic Travel Authorization system at https://www.eta.gov.lk

  1. Select 'Apply' tab from the menu
  2. A new window will open select 'I agree' to continue
  3. From the left menu, select applicable visa type for example 'Apply for an Individual' under 'Tourist ETA'
  4. Complete the application form with your personal detail, travel information, contact detail and declaration
  5. Click 'Next' to complete the application and proceed for payment

Frequently Asked Questions

How long can I stay in Sri Lanka on my tourist visa

Your tourist and business visas allows you to stay 30 days in Sri Lanka, while a transit visa is only for people traveling via Colombo onwards to a third country with stay of no more than 2 days.

Can I extend my tourism visa once I have reached Sri Lanka

Visitors who wish to stay in Sri Lanka for more than 30 days can apply for a visa extension. A short-term visit visa can be extended for up to 270 days from the date of arrival in three stages: an initial 30 days on entry, followed by a 60-day extension, and then two more extensions of 90 days each. To apply, visitors must submit their extension request in person at the visa section of the Department of Immigration & Emigration. There is also an option to apply for extension online.

What is approval ratio of Sri Lanka visa for Pakistani nationals

Pakistan is among the countries who can apply for electronic travel authority to Sri Lanka. The approval rate is almost as near to 100% except if rejected by the authorities due to any exceptional reason.

What is the meaning of single entry, double entry and multiple entry visa

A single entry visa allows you to enter the country only one time. Once you have entered the country, you cannot re-use same visa to re-enter the country even during the validity of visa. A double entry visa allows you to travel to the country two times (from another country) within the validity of your visa. Similarly a multiple entry visa allows you to travel unlimited time during the validity of your visa.

Can we get visa on arrival while traveling to Sri Lanka

Pakistan is not among the countries entitled for a visa on arrival in Sri Lanka. A pre-approved ETA is mandatory before you start your travel.
